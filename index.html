Frequently Asked
📅

Offer Validity

The Bumper Festive Bonanza is valid for leads added between 9 October and 9 November 2026, across all product categories. Leads approved on or before 14 November 2026 will be counted towards your final reward eligibility.

✅

Lead Eligibility

Only approved leads count towards your festive reward milestones. You must have at least 25 approved leads to qualify for a reward.

📦

Reward Claiming & Dispatch

Physical rewards will be delivered to your provided address within 30 working days after you submit your details through the reward claim form. Submit Your Details ↗

🛡️

Lead Verification & Disqualification

Any fraudulent, duplicate, or subsequently cancelled leads will be strictly disqualified during the final audit without prior notice.

🚫

Cash Alternative & Transfer

All festive bonanza rewards are non-transferable, non-negotiable, and strictly cannot be exchanged for cash.

🧾

Taxes & TDS

Any applicable taxes and TDS on rewards must be paid by the qualifying advisor.

⚖️

Final Decision

BankSathi (BS Fintech Private Limited) reserves the right to make the final decision on reward eligibility and campaign-related matters.

🎯

How are festive leads calculated?

All leads added between 9 October and 9 November 2026, across all product categories, will count towards your milestone rewards if approved by 14 November 2026.

Eligible categories include Credit Cards, Personal Loans, Bank Accounts, Insurance, Investments, Car Loans, and Demat Accounts.

🎁

When will I receive my reward?

Eligible winners will be notified via SMS, WhatsApp, and the BankSathi app after the offer ends.

Rewards will be delivered within 30 working days after you submit your delivery details through the Reward Claim Form ↗.

🏍️

Can I win multiple milestone rewards?

No. You will receive only one reward, based on the highest milestone you achieve.

For example, if you reach 1,000 approved leads, you'll win a Royal Enfield Bullet 350cc, not the rewards from previous milestones.

🔄

What happens if a customer cancels or returns a lead?

Only verified, fully active leads are counted. Disqualified or returned leads are automatically deducted during the final audit.

⚡

How do I track my progress?

Your lead count updates in real-time on your dashboard. Use the interactive calculator slider to see how many leads you need for the next reward!
